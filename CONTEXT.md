# TouchGrass

A software take on Brick. Distracting apps get Locked, and the only normal way out is to go outside and photograph real grass.

## Language

### Locking

**Locked**:
The state in which a set of apps is blocked on the phone. A phone is Locked while at least one Lock is active.
_Avoid_: Bricked, Grounded, Blocked, Shielded, Focus session

**Lock**:
A single period of being Locked, started by a Manual Lock, a Schedule, or a Budget running out. Every Lock locks the apps on one Block List.
_Avoid_: Session, Brick

**Block List**:
A named set of apps that a Lock blocks, such as "Social" or "Everything". One Block List is the default for Manual Locks.
_Avoid_: Mode, Selection, Category, Group

**Manual Lock**:
A Lock the user starts deliberately (from the app, Control Center, the Lock Screen, the Action button, Shortcuts, or a location trigger).
_Avoid_: Focus session, tap

**Schedule**:
A recurring time window that starts a Lock automatically, such as 9pm–7am. The Lock ends when the window ends.
_Avoid_: Downtime, Block schedule

**Budget**:
A daily allowance of minutes for the apps on a Block List that starts a Lock once it is used up. It refills at the Reset Time.
_Avoid_: Daily limit, Category budget, Goal plan

**Reset Time**:
The time of day when every Budget refills. Midnight by default; night owls might pick 4am.
_Avoid_: Day start, Midnight reset

### Strictness

**Strict**:
The default style, chosen at setup. Manual Locks last until Touch Grass, Touch Grass earns a Break rather than ending Schedule or Budget Locks, and loosening a setting only takes effect the next day.
_Avoid_: Hard mode, Brutal mode

**Flexible**:
The alternative style. Manual Locks can be given a duration, Touch Grass ends Locks outright, and settings change immediately. Any individual setting can be moved away from either style.
_Avoid_: Easy mode, Lenient mode

Settings are frozen while Locked, in both styles.

### Unlocking

**Touch Grass**:
The act of taking a live camera photo of real grass to end a Lock. It is the only normal way out. Other plants don't count, and the photo isn't kept.
_Avoid_: Challenge, Task, Unlock challenge, Proof

**Grass Check**:
The judgement of whether a Touch Grass photo really shows grass.
_Avoid_: Validation, Verification, Challenge review

**Grass Count**:
The running total of times a user has touched grass. It is the only history the app keeps.
_Avoid_: Streak, History, Stats

One Touch Grass applies to every active Lock at once. It ends a Manual Lock. For a Schedule or Budget Lock it earns a Break in Strict and ends the Lock in Flexible.

**Break**:
A short pause, 15 minutes by default, that Touch Grass earns in Strict. When it ends, the Schedule and Budget Locks it paused resume.
_Avoid_: Grace period, Cooldown, Unlocked grace

**Emergency Unlock**:
An escape hatch that ends every active Lock completely without Touching Grass. A user starts with 3 and can hold at most 3. Each one used comes back 30 days later.
_Avoid_: Emergency Unbrick, Override, Emergency unblock
